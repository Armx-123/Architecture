Here are 5 effective 'Formula Structures' for Pinterest titles for 'Architecture', along with the logic for their ranking:

1.  **[Number] [Style/Type] [Feature/Benefit]**

    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure is highly effective because it immediately provides key information to the user: how many examples are presented, what kind of architecture it is, and what makes it desirable or functional. It's direct, informative, and sets clear expectations. The number signifies value and breadth, the style targets a specific interest, and the feature/benefit highlights a desirable outcome.

2.  **[Number] [Style/Type] [Action/Goal]**

    *   **Logic/Formula:** Similar to the first, this formula excels by combining a quantifiable number of examples with a specific architectural style and an action or goal the user might have. "Ideas," "Designs," "Plans," and "Inspiration" all appeal to users actively seeking solutions or creative input. It directly addresses the user's search intent by offering actionable content related to their desired style.

3.  **[Style/Type] [Specific Feature/Adjective] [Adjective/Benefit] [House/Apartment/Plan]**

    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure leverages descriptive language to evoke emotion and appeal to aesthetic preferences. The combination of style with adjectives like "Modern," "Luxury," "Minimalist," "Cozy," or "Stunning" creates a strong visual and emotional hook. It's particularly effective when the adjectives are aspirational or promise a specific feeling or outcome, directly influencing a user's desire to click.

4.  **[Number] [Specific Architectural Style] [Specific Era/Characteristic] [Descriptive Adjective]**

    *   **Logic/Formula:** This formula is powerful for users with very specific interests, particularly historical or niche architectural styles. Including the era (e.g., "1880s," "1960s") or a defining characteristic (e.g., "Queen Anne," "Victorian") appeals to enthusiasts. The addition of a descriptive adjective like "Dream Home," "Remodel," or "For Sale" provides context and relevance, making the title highly targeted and attractive to a defined audience.

5.  **[Action/Goal] [Number] [Style/Type] [Benefit/Outcome]**

    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure prioritizes the user's potential action or desire upfront, followed by the specifics of the content. Phrases like "Discover," "Transform," or "Inspire" are strong calls to action. By leading with the benefit or goal, it immediately signals value and relevance to a user's search, making the subsequent details (number, style) even more compelling as they fulfill that initial promise.
