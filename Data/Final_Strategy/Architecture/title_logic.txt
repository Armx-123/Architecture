1.  **[Adjective] + [Noun] + [Style/Type] + [Descriptor]**
    *   **Logic:** This structure is highly effective because it immediately evokes a visual and aspirational feeling. The adjective grabs attention, the noun grounds the subject, and the style/type provides essential categorization. The descriptor adds further appeal and benefit, making it highly searchable and clickable.

2.  **[Number] + [Adjective] + [Noun] + [Purpose/Benefit]**
    *   **Logic:** This formula leverages the power of listicles and quantifiable value. The number promises digestible insights or inspiration. The adjective adds intrigue, the noun clarifies the subject, and the purpose/benefit directly addresses what the user will gain from clicking.

3.  **[Adjective] + [Architectural Element/Feature] + [Style/Era]**
    *   **Logic:** This structure excels by focusing on specific, desirable elements within architecture. The adjective creates immediate appeal, the specific element targets niche interests, and the style/era provides crucial context and searchability for users looking for particular aesthetics.

4.  **[Noun] + [Descriptor] + [Location/Style Focus]**
    *   **Logic:** This formula is effective for broad appeal and clear categorization. The noun establishes the core subject, the descriptor adds desirability or function, and the location/style focus makes it highly relevant and discoverable for users interested in specific regional or stylistic trends.

5.  **[Action Verb/Benefit] + [Adjective] + [Architectural Subject]**
    *   **Logic:** This structure is action-oriented and benefit-driven. The verb or benefit immediately signals value and what the user can achieve or experience. The adjective enhances the appeal of the architectural subject, making it compelling for users seeking inspiration or solutions.
