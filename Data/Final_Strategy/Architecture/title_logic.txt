Here are 5 successful 'Formula Structures' for Pinterest Architecture titles and the logic behind why they rank:

1.  **[Adjective] [Noun] [Keyword/Theme] Ideas**
    *   **Logic:** This structure is highly effective because it immediately captures attention with an appealing adjective, clearly defines the subject with a noun, and then broadens the scope with "Ideas" to suggest a collection of inspiration. It caters to users actively seeking visual suggestions and solutions.

2.  **[Number] [Adjective] [Keyword] Designs That Will Inspire You**
    *   **Logic:** The inclusion of a number promises a curated, digestible list, making the content seem more accessible and valuable. The adjective adds intrigue, and "Designs That Will Inspire You" directly addresses the user's goal of finding visually stimulating content.

3.  **[Adjective] [Keyword] Inspiration: [Specific Style/Feature]**
    *   **Logic:** This formula combines a broad, evocative adjective and the core keyword with a more specific descriptor. This allows users searching for general architecture inspiration to find it, while also appealing to those with a more refined interest in particular styles or features.

4.  **How to Achieve [Adjective] [Keyword] In Your Home**
    *   **Logic:** This structure targets a user actively looking for practical application and transformation. It positions the content as a helpful guide, promising actionable advice for achieving a desired aesthetic.

5.  **[Keyword] [Specific Type/Era] Architecture: [Key Element/Description]**
    *   **Logic:** This formula is highly targeted and SEO-friendly. It clearly identifies the main subject (keyword) and then narrows it down with a specific type or era, followed by a compelling descriptor. This appeals to users with a precise interest and signals relevance for specific search queries.
