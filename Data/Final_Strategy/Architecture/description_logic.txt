## Hook Styles:

1.  **Question-Based Hook:**
    *   Template: "Looking for [aspirational outcome]? Discover stunning [architecture type/style] ideas!"
    *   Logical Flow: Pique curiosity by posing a relatable problem or desire, then immediately offer a solution or inspiration.

2.  **Benefit-Driven Hook:**
    *   Template: "Transform your space with breathtaking [architectural feature/concept] inspiration."
    *   Logical Flow: Highlight the immediate value or positive impact the user will gain by engaging with the pin.

3.  **Intrigue/Curiosity Hook:**
    *   Template: "Unlock the secrets to [unique architectural element/design principle]."
    *   Logical Flow: Create a sense of mystery or exclusivity, hinting at hidden knowledge or exclusive insights.

## CTA (Call to Action) Structure:

*   **Template:** "Tap to explore more [relevant keyword/inspiration]!"
*   **Logical Flow:** Direct, action-oriented, and reinforces the core benefit/content of the pin. It clearly tells the user what to do and what they will get by doing it.
