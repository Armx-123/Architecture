Here are 5 successful Pinterest title formula structures for 'interior' and why they rank:

1.  **[Adjective] + [Noun] + [Benefit/Result]**
    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure immediately hooks the user with an evocative adjective, clearly states the subject (the noun), and then promises a desirable outcome or solution.
    *   **Why it ranks:** It's highly descriptive, sparks curiosity, and directly addresses the user's potential aspirations or problems. The combination of visual appeal (adjective) and practical value (benefit) is compelling.

2.  **[Number] + [Adjective] + [Noun] + [Theme/Style]**
    *   **Logic/Formula:** This formula provides a quantifiable promise (number), sets a mood (adjective), identifies the core subject (noun), and then provides specific context or genre.
    *   **Why it ranks:** Numbers create a sense of organization and completeness (e.g., "5 ways," "10 ideas"). The adjective adds emotional appeal, and the theme/style helps users quickly filter for their specific interests.

3.  **[Question/Problem] + [Solution/Topic]**
    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure directly engages the user by acknowledging a common question or problem they might have and then offers the content as the solution or answer.
    *   **Why it ranks:** It's highly relatable and instantly taps into the user's need or curiosity. When a title poses a question, the user instinctively wants to click to find the answer.

4.  **[Noun] + [Action Verb] + [Goal/Outcome]**
    *   **Logic/Formula:** This formula presents the core subject (noun), explains what the content will help the user *do* (action verb), and clearly states the positive result they will achieve.
    *   **Why it ranks:** It's practical and aspirational. It tells the user not just *what* the pin is about, but what they can *achieve* by engaging with it, making it feel actionable and valuable.

5.  **[Adjective/Descriptor] + [Noun] + [Inspiration/Idea Type]**
    *   **Logic/Formula:** This structure uses an adjective to describe the feeling or aesthetic of the noun, clearly states the noun, and then specifies the type of inspirational content being offered.
    *   **Why it ranks:** It's strong on visual appeal and sets an expectation for the kind of content. It caters to users who are browsing for aesthetic inspiration and looking for curated collections of ideas.
