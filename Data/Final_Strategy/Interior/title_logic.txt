Here are 5 best 'Formula Structures' for Pinterest titles based on successful examples for 'Interior', with their logic:

1.  **[Adjective] + [Noun] + for [Purpose/Style]**
    *   **Logic:** This structure is highly effective because it immediately tells the user what kind of interior they are looking at (e.g., "Cozy Living Room") and then specifies its intended use or aesthetic (e.g., "for Small Apartments" or "for a Minimalist Home"). It's descriptive, aspirational, and targets a specific user need or interest.

2.  **[Number] + [Adjective] + [Interior Element] + Ideas**
    *   **Logic:** This formula taps into the user's desire for curated inspiration and actionable advice. The number provides a sense of comprehensive content, the adjective adds appeal, and "Ideas" signals that the pin will offer solutions and visual concepts. It's a direct answer to a user's search for inspiration.

3.  **[Keywords of Main Feature/Style] + [Type of Space] + Inspiration**
    *   **Logic:** This structure is excellent for SEO and direct discovery. It prioritizes the most important search terms first, clearly identifying the core subject matter (e.g., "Boho Bedroom"). "Inspiration" is a strong keyword that users often search for when looking for new ideas.

4.  **How to [Achieve Result] with [Interior Element/Style]**
    *   **Logic:** This is a problem/solution or aspiration-driven formula. It directly addresses a user's query or desire by promising guidance on how to achieve a specific outcome using a particular interior approach. It's highly actionable and targets users looking for advice and tutorials.

5.  **[Keywords of Core Aesthetic] + [Room Type] + Decor**
    *   **Logic:** This is a straightforward and highly searchable structure. It combines the essential elements that users look for: the overarching style they're interested in and the specific room they want to decorate. "Decor" is a direct and widely used search term.
