Here are the 5 best 'Formula Structures' for titles, based on the provided data, along with their logic:

1.  **"Number + [Adjective/Benefit] + [Noun Phrase/Topic] + Ideas"**
    *   **Logic:** This structure directly promises a curated list of solutions. The number signals a comprehensive guide, the adjective/benefit highlights value (e.g., Affordable, Cheap, Stunning, Easy), and the noun phrase clearly states the subject matter. "Ideas" confirms it's a collection of suggestions.
    *   **Why it Ranks:** It's highly effective because it sets clear expectations for the user looking for inspiration and practical solutions. It's direct, benefit-driven, and quantifiable.

2.  **"Number + [Adjective/Benefit] + [Noun Phrase/Topic] + For + [Specific Use/Outcome]"**
    *   **Logic:** Similar to the first, but adds a layer of specificity by indicating *what* the ideas are for or *what they achieve*. This hones in on the user's specific need or goal.
    *   **Why it Ranks:** This structure is powerful because it moves beyond general inspiration to targeted solutions. It directly answers the "why" behind the user's search.

3.  **"[Adjective/Benefit] + [Noun Phrase/Topic] + Ideas + For + [Specific Use/Outcome]"**
    *   **Logic:** This formula prioritizes the benefit or adjective immediately, followed by the topic and its application. It omits the explicit number, implying a comprehensive list or a focus on quality over quantity, and then specifies the context.
    *   **Why it Ranks:** It's effective by leading with the desired outcome or quality, making it highly clickable for users with specific goals. It feels more curated and focused.

4.  **"Number + [Noun Phrase/Topic] + Ideas"**
    *   **Logic:** This is a very straightforward and effective structure. It clearly states the quantity and the topic, with "Ideas" indicating a list of suggestions.
    *   **Why it Ranks:** Simplicity and clarity are key. Users understand exactly what they're going to get: a numbered list of ideas about a specific subject. It's highly scannable.

5.  **"DIY + [Noun Phrase/Topic] + Ideas"**
    *   **Logic:** This formula directly appeals to users looking for do-it-yourself projects. It clearly identifies the content as hands-on and actionable.
    *   **Why it Ranks:** It's a strong contender because it targets a specific user intent – those who want to create things themselves. It immediately signals actionable content.
